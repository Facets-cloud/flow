flow is a small CLI that gives each piece of work a named Claude Code or Codex session. The session survives the tab, keeps working while you sleep, and asks you on Slack when it needs a decision. You talk; the agent runs flow.

It pushes nothing and spends nothing without a yes from you. The gates are in the brief you write. It is not cheap in tokens; the numbers are in the README.
